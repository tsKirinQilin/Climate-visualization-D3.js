Methodology

How the data is used

Realtime

Detailed selected-point conditions come from OpenWeather. The global temperature, precipitation, cloud and wind field comes from Open-Meteo.

Short-range analytics

The 24-hour and 7-day charts use Open-Meteo forecast data. They are forecasts, not observations.

Seasonal forecast

Monthly outlooks use the ECMWF SEAS5 ensemble mean through Open-Meteo. They describe broad regional tendencies rather than exact weather on a particular day.

Anomaly

A temperature anomaly is the model forecast minus the model's long-term climatology. +2 °C means warmer than that model baseline, not necessarily 2 °C warmer than last year.

Historical comparisons

Past-period comparisons used by Climate AI come from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API and are calculated on the server.

Climate AI

Gemini interprets the question and requests application tools. Numerical weather values and comparisons are produced by the backend services; the model explains the returned data.
